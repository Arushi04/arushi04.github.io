Hi! I am Arushi

I’m a Senior Hardware Analytics Engineer and Tech Lead at Meta, working on hardware reliability, fleet power, silicon analytics, and aging studies across large-scale systems.

My work focuses on understanding how silicon behaves in the real world — how power, performance, reliability, and aging show up across the fleet, and how data can help detect issues earlier and make better engineering decisions.

Work at Meta

Power, capacity, and AI infrastructure planning

  • Built rack power datasets, monitoring tools, and forecasting analysis to identify unused power headroom, understand peak demand, and assess whether growth or high-performance settings could exceed local power limits.
  • Developed workload-aware power models and component-level characterization for AI infrastructure, informing rack-budget decisions, identifying capacity opportunities, and assessing power-capping and training-performance risks.
  • Built a cross-team power analytics assistant that routes natural-language questions to expert-owned data workflows, unifies access to budgeting and capacity insights, and validates answers against source-of-truth queries.

Silicon characterization, validation, and lifecycle monitoring

  • Built reusable silicon-test pipelines and dashboards that standardized power, performance, stability, and error metrics, automated validation reporting, and supported production operating-point qualification across lab characterization, validation monitoring, manufacturing-test analysis, and memory power/thermal characterization.
  • Developed longitudinal ASIC telemetry, standardized health datasets, and on-chip sensor decoding to enable silicon-aging studies, failure-correlation analysis, and lab-to-fleet traceability.

Fleet reliability and interconnect diagnostics

  • Developed multivariate SerDes classification, telemetry analytics, and diagnostic dashboards to identify marginal interconnects, investigate lane-level failures, and support hardware qualification and repair decisions.
  • Expanded fleet and ASIC health reporting and analyzed hardware failures, repair coverage, and incident trends to surface reliability gaps and guide engineering investigations.

AI-assisted diagnostics and trusted engineering data platforms

  • Developed and evaluated NLP/LLM approaches for hardware-error classification, reducing dependence on manual specialist review.
  • Built a firmware intelligence system that converts heterogeneous vendor release documents into structured summaries and searchable version histories with natural-language querying.
  • Developed an automated usage- and cost-based review process for hardware-health pipelines, removing unused processing and establishing recurring reviews to improve data reliability and compute efficiency.
